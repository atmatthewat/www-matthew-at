RadioID Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

RadioID is a commercial application developed and published by Matthew Kaufman, provided on an as-is basis.

Information Collection and Use

RadioID processes audio from your device's microphone in real time solely to perform its core function. Audio is never stored on your device, recorded, or transmitted to any remote server or third party.

RadioID does not integrate any third-party services, analytics frameworks, or tracking tools that could be used to identify you or collect personal information.

Permissions

RadioID requests microphone access at runtime. The microphone is accessed only while the app is actively running and only for the purpose of real-time audio processing described above. You may revoke microphone permission at any time through your device's system settings.
